How to use

1. Put it on a MIDI track
It is an instrument with its own sub. Press play in Ableton and it plays its 4-bar line in time.
2. Choose KEY and SCALE
The line sits in octave 0 of Ableton (30-65 Hz), mono. The keys in blue are the ones Mindshake uses most.
3. GENERATE and VARIATION
GENERATE makes a new line in A A' A A'' form. VARIATION only changes bars 2 and 4. DENSITY, GATE, MOVE and KICK reshape the same idea.
4. Edit the grid
Click to add, move or remove a note: it stays locked. Shift+click locks or unlocks a step. Locked steps survive GENERATE.
5. Shape the sound
Pick a sound from the list or press NEW SOUND for one of your own; SINE is always there. DRIVE adds warmth without getting louder. MID adds presence at 280 Hz so it reads on small speakers. FILTER and DECAY close it up.
6. Your own notes
Send MIDI notes and it plays them; the pattern waits until you stop.
7. Drag it
Drag the bar at the bottom into Ableton: MIDI or audio (choose above).
